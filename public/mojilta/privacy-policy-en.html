1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Moa Lab (“we”) handles information about users of Mojilta (“the app”). Mojilta is a read-only reader for TXT and Markdown files you provide. It leaves the original files unchanged.

This policy applies to the app. For information about browsing the website, including the Mojilta product page, see the Website Privacy Policy (Japanese).

2. Files you read and information stored on your device

You can use the app without registering or signing in. The app uses read-only access to files you select through your operating system’s standard file picker or a similar method. It processes text on your device for reading, searching, and text encoding conversion, without writing changes back to the original file. It does not persistently store the text or nearby passages as part of your reading history.

To show recently opened files and let you resume reading, the app stores the following information on your device. File names and similar information may contain personal information, depending on the names you choose.

  • References used to reopen files, file names, file formats, and whether the app can continue to access each file
  • The date and time you last read a file, reading position and progress, character count, and selected text encoding
  • Operating system references used to reopen files on iOS

The app also stores settings on your device, including font, text size, line spacing, margins, appearance, accent color, and display language. When you resume reading, the app reads the original file again instead of using a saved copy of its text.

3. Copying text and using external services

When you copy text, the app passes the selected text to the operating system’s clipboard. How the text is stored or sent after you paste it into another app, and how the clipboard retains or syncs it, depend on your actions and the settings and rules of the operating system and services you use.

You can read downloaded files offline if your purchased access is stored on the device. An internet connection is required to make a purchase, verify a purchase for the first time, or restore a purchase. When you open a cloud file you have not downloaded, the file provider may require a connection or sign-in. The provider’s rules govern how its service handles information.

The app does not load images in Markdown or automatically retrieve external images. If you tap an HTTP or HTTPS link in a document, the app opens it in an external app. The destination service’s rules govern communications with that service and how it handles information.

4. Purchase verification and data sent to servers

The app uses RevenueCat to verify and restore purchases and manage purchase information. RevenueCat and the store you use process anonymous identifiers, product and transaction information, purchase status, and technical information such as operating system and app versions. You do not need a Mojilta account. Payments go through the store. To restore a purchase, use the same store account you used to make it.

The app does not send file contents, file names, or reading history to RevenueCat. The app has no features that send text, file references, or clipboard contents to our servers or sync documents to the cloud. Advertising identifier collection and additional diagnostic reporting are disabled. The app uses neither advertising SDKs nor analytics SDKs that track document reading activity.

For information about how external services handle data, see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

What you do with text after copying it, communications with file providers, following external links, and sending support emails are handled separately as described in the relevant sections of this policy.

5. Deleting history and data

You can clear the history of recently opened files in the app. Clearing history removes its entries. It does not delete the original files, revoke access permissions managed by the operating system, or remove references used to reopen files on iOS.

Deleting history or uninstalling the app does not delete purchase information managed by the store or RevenueCat. For questions about purchase information, contact us using the details below or contact the store where you made your purchase.

To manage or delete original files, use your device’s file management tools or your file provider’s service. Use your operating system’s tools to manage data on your device, such as app settings. To manage or delete content you copied and saved in another app, use that app. We cannot remotely view or delete information stored on your device.

6. Information we handle when you contact us

If you contact us by email, we handle your sender address, information you provide such as your name, the message contents, and any attachments. We use this information to respond, communicate with you, verify your identity, and investigate problems. Please include only the personal information or document contents needed for your inquiry.

Opening an email link does not send your message to us. The message is sent when you choose to send it in your email app or a similar service. We use an email provider to send, receive, and store email.

7. Information management and sharing with third parties

We take necessary and appropriate safeguards, such as access restrictions, for purchase and inquiry information we handle. We retain it for as long as needed to verify and restore purchases, respond to inquiries, investigate problems, or fulfill similar purposes, or as required by law. We seek to delete information that is no longer needed.

We do not provide personal data to third parties without the person’s consent, except when using service providers to the extent necessary for our operations, when disclosure is based on law, or in similar cases. Information held by external services is subject to each provider’s retention and deletion practices. When providing information to third parties in other countries, we take any steps required by law, including providing information and obtaining consent.

8. Requests for disclosure and other inquiries

Use the contact details below to ask about disclosure, correction, deletion, suspension of use, or security safeguards for information we hold about you. We verify your identity as necessary and respond in accordance with applicable law. We cannot view or delete data on your device that we have not obtained.

9. Changes to this policy

We revise this policy when our handling of information or applicable law changes. We announce significant changes on this page, in the app, or through similar means. Where required by law, we give advance notice or obtain consent.
