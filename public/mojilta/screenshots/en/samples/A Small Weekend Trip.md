# A Small Weekend Trip

You do not have to travel far for a different kind of morning.
A weekend plan: walk beside the river, then stop for tea at a small cafe.

## A morning walk

From the station, head for the **riverside path**.
Take your time. Stop at a side street or a shop that catches your eye.

- Watch the light on the water from the bridge
- Choose a freshly baked loaf of bread
- Open something to read on a bench

> Leave some space in the day.
> Time for a detour is part of the trip.

## What to bring

- A small notebook and a pen
- Water and a folding umbrella
- A TXT story and your walking notes

**One thing to decide: write a letter before lunch.**

## Plans for the afternoon

| Time | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 10:00 | Walk slowly beside the river |
| 11:00 | Have tea by a window |
| 12:00 | Find somewhere for lunch |
| 14:00 | Write a few lines about the day |

## Notes for when you get home

Keep just three scenes from the morning.
There is no need to write a grand travel story. Write down what you want to remember.

```text
Blue sky reflected in a puddle
A nod from someone on the bridge
A white cup beside the window
```

The next time you feel like going out, continue these notes.
